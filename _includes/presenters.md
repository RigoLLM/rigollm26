## Workshop Program

Please check the [program]({{ '/program.html' | relative_url }}) for your session and time slot.
All times are Hong Kong Time (HKT).

## Talk Length

| Talk type | Total slot | Suggested split |
|---|---|---|
| Keynote | 60 minutes | about 45–50 min talk + 10–15 min Q&A |
| High-impact work talk | 15 minutes | about 12 min talk + 3 min Q&A |
| Workshop paper | 15 minutes | about 12 min talk + 3 min Q&A |
| Extended abstract | 8 minutes | 5 min talk + 3 min Q&A |

Session chairs will keep time strictly so every session stays on schedule. Please rehearse
your talk to fit within its slot.

## In-Person Presentation

In-person speakers are expected to present on site at
**{{ site.workshop.venue }}, {{ site.workshop.location }}**.

- **Bring your own laptop** with your slides, plus any adapter you need to connect to the
  projector (HDMI is the safest choice).
- **Arrive at the room during the break before your session.** Introduce yourself to the
  session chair and test your laptop with the projector.
- **Have a backup copy** of your slides (PDF on a USB drive) in case your laptop has problems.

## Remote Presentation

Remote presentation is allowed,
but must be arranged with the organizers in advance. If you cannot attend in person, please
email us as soon as possible.

Approved remote speakers will:

- **Present live over Zoom** at their scheduled time. The organizers will send the Zoom link
  to remote speakers before the workshop. The workshop itself is not streamed.
- **Take questions live** over Zoom after the talk.
- **Record a backup video** of the full talk in advance and send it to the organizers by
  **November 8, 2026**. We will play the video only if the live connection fails, so you
  should still plan to present live.

Backup video requirements:

- Within your talk's time limit (talk portion only, without Q&A)
- A complete recording of your talk
- A common video format, such as MP4
- Clear audio and readable slides
- Shared as a downloadable link (for example, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, or YouTube)

Please remember that your time slot is in Hong Kong Time and convert it to your local time zone.

## Before the Workshop

1. Check your session and time slot in the [program]({{ '/program.html' | relative_url }}).
2. Prepare a talk that fits within your slot, leaving time for Q&A.
3. **In-person speakers:** bring your laptop, adapter, and a backup PDF of your slides.
4. **Remote speakers (pre-approved only):** send your backup video by November 8, 2026, and
   test Zoom, screen sharing, audio, and video beforehand.
5. Find your session chair during the break before your session.
